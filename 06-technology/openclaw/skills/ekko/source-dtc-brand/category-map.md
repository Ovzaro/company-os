# Category Map — Ekko's sourcing universe

The product lanes Ekko hunts within, with celeb angles. Use it to vary the batch mix and spark search
ideas; it is **not** a green-light list. **Every entry still goes through the full qualification gate**
(true DTC, own branded site, live/functional checkout, no manufacturers) and the **celeb No-Go check**.

**Filters already applied to this map:**
1. **Howie No-Go removed** (this list is Howie-oriented). Removed items are noted per section. Brooke
   has *extra* restrictions — see the bottom.
2. **Services & non-DTC removed** (for now) — finance, insurance, travel booking/airlines/hotels,
   restaurants & delivery, streaming, telehealth, fitness/education/productivity apps, auto services,
   **plus mega-brand categories that aren't DTC** (smartphones/computing, OTC pharma, game consoles,
   streaming devices, smart speakers). This map is **true-DTC products only** — if it isn't a brand
   that sells its own product from its own site, it's out.

## 🥤 Food & Beverage
- **Hydration:** still water, alkaline, electrolyte, infused/flavored, sparkling, water delivery.
- **Energy & functional drinks:** energy (non-alc), pre-workout, nootropic/focus, adaptogen, kombucha, coconut water.
- **Coffee & tea:** ground/whole bean, single-serve pods, cold brew, canned coffee, green/herbal tea, matcha, tea subscriptions.
- **Protein & meal replacement:** protein powders, RTD protein shakes, meal-replacement bars, weight-management shakes, plant-based protein. *(Brooke No-Go: protein powder/bars.)*
- **Healthy snacks:** granola/protein bars, nut & trail mix, popcorn, rice cakes, veggie chips, dried fruit, jerky/meat snacks, crackers & dips. *(Food doesn't count toward an H&W brief unless Jacob asks for food.)*
- **Candy & gum:** gum, breath mints, hard candy, chocolate (non-alcohol), sugar-free candy.
- **General-wellness supplements:** multivitamins, vitamin C/immune, probiotics, omega-3/fish oil, magnesium, collagen, elderberry/herbal.

## 🧼 Personal Care & Hygiene
- **Oral care:** electric toothbrushes, toothpaste, whitening strips/kits, mouthwash, water flossers, floss, tongue scrapers.
- **Skin care:** face wash/cleansers, moisturizers, SPF, anti-aging creams, acne treatments, eye cream, toners/serums, sheet masks, lip balm.
- **Hair care (Howie comedy angle):** shampoo & conditioner, dry shampoo, hair masks, scalp care, hair-growth serums, anti-dandruff.
- **Body care:** body wash/shower gel, bar soap, loofah/shower tools, body lotion/butter, exfoliating scrubs.
- **Deodorant & fragrance:** antiperspirant/deodorant, natural deodorant, men's cologne, body spray.
- **Shaving & grooming:** razors (disposable & reusable), shaving cream/gel, electric shavers/trimmers, beard oil/balm (ironic Howie angle), aftershave, grooming kits.
- **Nail care:** clippers/tools, polish, strengtheners, at-home manicure kits.

## 🏥 Health & Wellness
- **Monitoring devices:** ⚠️ medical — smart thermometers, BP monitors, pulse oximeters, smart scales/body comp. *(Glucose monitors & ECG = medical devices — usually skip.)*
- **Sleep & recovery:** sleep-tracking devices, white-noise machines, sleep masks, weighted blankets, cooling/heating sleep pads, melatonin/sleep supplements.
- **Physical therapy & recovery:** massage guns, foam rollers, compression sleeves, posture correctors, TENS/EMS devices, ice/heat packs, knee/back braces.
- **Sanitization & germ protection (germaphobe gold):** UV-C sanitizing wands, UV phone sanitizers, UV sterilizer boxes, touchless soap dispensers, touchless faucets/fixtures, contactless door openers.
  - ❌ Removed (Howie No-Go): **antibacterial wipes** (cleaning).
- **Hearing health:** OTC hearing aids, hearing amplifiers, ear-cleaning tools.
- **Vision health:** eye/lubricating drops, contact-lens solution, eye-massage tools.
  - ❌ Removed (Howie No-Go): **blue-light-blocking glasses** (eyeglasses).
- **First aid:** kits, bandages/wound care, antiseptic sprays, medical tape/wraps.
