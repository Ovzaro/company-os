# Category Map — Brooke Burke (write as Joe Theismann)

Brooke's hunting universe — age-positive beauty, wellness, and lifestyle. Same rules as always:
**every entry still goes through the full qualification gate** (true DTC, own branded site, live
checkout, no manufacturers). This map is **true-DTC products only**, already cleaned of Brooke's
No-Go items, services, and non-DTC mega-brands (see the removed list at the bottom).

> Companion to `category-map.md` (Howie's map). Use the one that matches the batch's celeb.

---

## 🏋️ Fitness & recovery (gear — not apparel/shoes)
Resistance bands & loop sets, dumbbells & adjustable weights, kettlebells, Pilates equipment
(reformers, rings, balls), stability/balance balls & bosu trainers, foam rollers, massage guns /
percussion therapy, compression boots & joint sleeves (knee, ankle, elbow, calf, wrist),
red-light therapy panels, home cardio (treadmills,
bikes, ellipticals), rowing machines, mini trampolines / rebounders, gym bags & equipment storage,
water bottles & shaker cups, yoga/meditation accessories (blocks, straps, bolsters), exercise mats.
- ⚠️ **Yoga mats** are OK only if *not* the main/only SKU (Brooke No-Go on yoga-mat-as-hero).

## 🌿 Health, wellness & supplements
Women's multivitamins, bone & joint (calcium, glucosamine), probiotics / gut health, adaptogens &
stress support, sleep aids (melatonin, magnesium), energy & focus, hydration & electrolyte mixes,
greens powders & superfoods, omega-3 / fish oil, immune support, heart-health supplements.

## 💆 Beauty & skincare (age-positive — Brooke's core)
Anti-aging serums (retinol, peptides), moisturizers for mature skin, eye creams, vitamin C /
brightening, sunscreen / SPF, neck & décolleté treatments, cleansers & toners, masks & exfoliants,
LED light-therapy masks, microcurrent / facial-sculpting devices, cosmetics (foundation/concealer for
mature skin), lip & eye makeup, hair care for thinning/aging hair, hair color & root touch-ups,
hair-growth serums & supplements, body lotions & firming creams, hand & nail care, fragrance/perfume,
**topical** collagen.

## 🥗 Nutrition & food (product brands)
Functional teas & coffees, non-alcoholic beverages, kombucha & functional sodas, healthy cooking
ingredients (oils, seasonings), better-for-you chips & crackers, jerky / whole-food snacks.
- *(Food doesn't count toward an H&W brief unless Jacob asks for food. Avoid protein-powder/bar
  products — Brooke No-Go.)*

## 👗 Fashion & accessories (no shoes, no athleisure, no intimates/shapewear)
Everyday women's apparel, elevated / occasion wear, denim, loungewear & sleepwear, swimwear, fine &
fashion jewelry, watches (DTC watch brands), handbags, scarves & accessories.

## 🏠 Home, family & motherhood
Home organization & storage, bedding & linens, home fragrance & candles, cleaning & home-care
products, air purifiers & humidifiers, blenders & juicers, air fryers & healthy-cooking appliances,
cookware & kitchen tools, kids' vitamins / family wellness, parenting & baby products (non-diaper),
DTC smart-home / family tech.
- *(Cleaning & air purifiers are Howie No-Go but fine for Brooke.)*

## ✈️ Travel & lifestyle (products only)
Luggage & travel bags, travel accessories, outdoor & patio living, books / journals / planners
(wellness, self-help).

meal-replacement / smoothie powders, menopause & hormone-support supplements, CBD wellness, at-home
health/diagnostic testing kits, non-invasive aesthetic injectables (Botox / fillers / lasers),
shapewear, intimates & lingerie, eyewear / sunglasses, footwear (heels, flats, comfort), training shoes /
cross-trainers, recovery sandals / slides, activewear leggings & sports bras, athleisure tops & sets,
workout outerwear, low-alcohol beverages.

**Services removed (not DTC):** fitness apps & on-demand workout streaming, personal training /
coaching subscriptions, meal-kit & prepared-meal delivery, smoothie/juice subscriptions,
intermittent-fasting / diet programs, wellness resorts & spa retreats, hotels & hospitality, cruise
lines, retirement/financial planning, life & health insurance / Medicare, telehealth, women's-health
platforms (40+), estate / family-planning services.

**Non-DTC / mega-brand removed:** fitness trackers & smartwatches, heart-rate monitors, family SUVs /
lifestyle vehicles.
