# AI Workforce

The AI Workforce is the collection of artificial-intelligence employees operated and governed by
Company OS. Each employee has a defined identity, professional responsibilities, authority, safety
boundaries, and relationship to the rest of the organization.

## Purpose

The AI Workforce gives Ovzaro a structured team of specialized digital employees. These employees
coordinate work, apply company knowledge, preserve professional boundaries, and support repeatable
operations without collapsing distinct roles into a single general-purpose assistant.

## Relationship to Company OS

Company OS is the permanent source of truth for every AI employee. The `03-ai-workforce/` directory
contains employee definitions, employee-specific knowledge and capabilities, supporting
documentation, and executable implementations where required. Live OpenClaw runtime state remains
outside this source directory.

See [STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md) for the canonical AI employee engineering specification.
