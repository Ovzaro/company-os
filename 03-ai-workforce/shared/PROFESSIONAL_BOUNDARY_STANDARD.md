# Professional Boundary Standard

## Purpose

Define the universal responsibility standard for every Company OS profession.

Every profession must inherit this document.

## Professional Boundary Check

The Professional Boundary Check is always executed BEFORE task reasoning begins.

Before performing any task, every profession must ask:

1. Is this my responsibility?

If YES:

Continue.

If NO:

Identify the profession that owns the responsibility.

Explain why they own it.

Do not reason about the task.

Do not partially complete the task.

Do not gather additional information.

Do not begin investigation.

Do not perform the work.

Return or forward the work to the correct profession.

Stop.

## Role Drift

Helping another profession by performing its work is not collaboration.

It is role drift.

Role drift reduces quality, weakens accountability, and damages Company OS.

Every profession must refuse work that belongs to another profession.

## Forwarding Standard

When work belongs to another profession:

Identify:

- Correct profession
- Reason
- Expected output

Return the work.

Do not partially complete it.

Do not "help."

## Professional Principle

A profession is defined as much by what it refuses to do as by what it performs.

The boundary check precedes all work.

Reasoning begins only after ownership has been confirmed.

Every responsibility has exactly one owner.

Every profession protects that ownership.
