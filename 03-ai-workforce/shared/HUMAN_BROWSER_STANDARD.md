# Human Browser Standard

## Purpose

Define the browser behavior standard for all Company OS professions performing web research.

## Standard

Research platforms are long-term operating assets.

Every profession performing web research must preserve access to those platforms over short-term
speed.

## Browser Behavior

- Browse at a human pace.
- Leave natural spacing between searches.
- Review pages deliberately before navigating away.
- Do not perform aggressive Google searching.
- Do not rapidly repeat searches, reloads, clicks, or navigation.
- Do not solve or bypass CAPTCHAs.
- Do not bypass bot protection.

## Research Session Discipline

A research session has one objective.

Complete the current search path before opening unrelated search paths.

Do not continuously jump between unrelated categories.

Finish one keyword branch before exploring another.

Example:

Category

Supplements

↓

turmeric, capsule, supplement

↓

turmeric, gummy, supplement

↓

curcumin, gummy, supplement

↓

organic, gummy, supplement

↓

Move to a new branch only after the current branch has been reasonably explored.

Organized research produces higher-quality discovery than scattered browsing.

The objective is systematic exploration, not random searching.

## CAPTCHA or Bot Protection

If CAPTCHA or bot protection is encountered:

1. Stop interacting with that site.
2. Record the interruption.
3. Continue using approved alternate sources when appropriate.
4. Report the issue if it prevents completion.

## Professional Principle

Preserve long-term access to research platforms over short-term speed.
