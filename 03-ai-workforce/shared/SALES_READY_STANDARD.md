# Sales Ready Standard

## Purpose

Define exactly what "Sales Ready" means for Company OS.

This document defines the finish line.

Every future Jayce improvement must support this standard.

Every future Shen Certification 2 decision will eventually certify against this standard.

This document does not implement behavior changes.

## Sales Ready Definition

A company is Sales Ready when Sales can confidently begin outreach without needing to investigate,
repair, interpret, or complete the record.

Sales Ready means the record contains:

- A qualified company.
- A normalized website.
- A clear category.
- A named decision maker when one can be reasonably identified.
- A clean communication route.
- A complete Communication Package.
- No investigation notes in CRM-facing fields.
- No ambiguous responsibility left unresolved.

## Contact Standard

Sales Ready contact intelligence identifies the strongest reachable path into the company.

The preferred outcome is a named decision maker with:

- First Name
- Last Name
- Real Title
- Verified Email when available
- Verified Phone when available

If direct contact information is unavailable after reasonable investigation, the named decision maker
remains the contact and verified company communication routes are used to reach that person.

If no reliable named decision maker can be identified after reasonable exhaustion, a fallback company
contact may be used only when it is clean, professional, and clearly formatted.

## Communication Route Standard

Sales Ready communication routes are verified and usable.

Preferred route order:

1. Verified direct email.
2. Verified direct phone.
3. Verified company email.
4. Preferred verified local company phone.
5. Best verified company phone.
6. Verified contact form.

If no verified communication route exists, the package must explicitly state:

```text
No verified company communication route found.
```

## CRM Cleanliness Standard

Sales Ready records must be clean enough to become permanent CRM records.

CRM-facing fields must not contain:

- Investigation notes.
- Reasoning.
- Confidence language.
- Search history.
- Evidence summaries.
- LinkedIn research.
- RocketReach notes.
- Explanations that belong only to the investigator.

CRM-facing fields must contain only execution-ready information.

## Completion Standard

Sales Ready does not mean perfect information.

Sales Ready means every required professional effort has been completed, every missing item has been
made explicit, and Sales can act without guessing.

The record is not Sales Ready if:

- The company has not been qualified.
- The company is a duplicate.
- The decision maker is unclear without explanation.
- Contact fields contain placeholders or investigation notes.
- The communication route is missing silently.
- The CRM package requires Viktor to investigate, infer, repair, or invent information.

## Professional Principle

Sales Ready is the finish line.

The standard is not whether a record can be entered into CRM.

The standard is whether Sales can use the record immediately, professionally, and confidently.
