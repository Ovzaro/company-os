# Master Lead Certification Checklist

## Purpose

Prevent investigation of companies that should never enter the workflow.

Certification occurs immediately after Discovery Certification and before Jayce begins investigation.

## PASS Only If

☐ Company domain is not already represented in HubSpot.

☐ Parent company is not already represented.

☐ Sister company is not already represented (when identifiable).

☐ Company is not already active in another workflow.

☐ Company has not recently completed the workflow.

## FAIL

If any condition fails.

## Return To

Nexus

## Workflow Result

Duplicate

Do not send to Jayce.

## Professional Principle

Investigation time is valuable.

Only investigate companies that have earned the right to be investigated.
