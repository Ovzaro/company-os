# Naming Methodology

The benchmark set supplied for this project clusters around a small number of durable patterns:

- One to three syllables, with two syllables the strongest default.
- Four to eight letters, usually with alternating consonant and vowel sounds.
- Familiar phonemes arranged in an unfamiliar word: `Figma`, `Vanta`, and `Nvidia` feel novel without requiring spelling instructions.
- Strong openings and clean endings such as `-a`, `-ia`, `-o`, `-is`, `-on`, and `-ar`.
- One visually simple token with no punctuation, numerals, or forced capitalization.
- Either a vivid real-word association (`Apple`, `Stripe`, `Ramp`) or an invented/modified construction broad enough to escape a product category.

The generator therefore uses curated roots, meanings, and transformations across invented, Latin-inspired, Greek-inspired, astronomy, mythology, scientific, blended, and modified-real-word strategies. It enumerates deliberate combinations; it does not randomly assemble letters. Ranking measures consonant-vowel balance, syllable count, pronounceability, spelling simplicity, visual repetition, familiar phonetic clusters, premium endings, global usability, and category independence.
