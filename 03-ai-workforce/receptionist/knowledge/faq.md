# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Purpose

This document contains the approved responses the Receptionist may use when answering common customer questions.

If a question is not answered in this document or another approved company knowledge source, the Receptionist should gather information and escalate rather than invent an answer.

---

# What does your company do?

We design and implement custom AI automation systems that help businesses reduce repetitive work, improve efficiency, and create better customer experiences. Every solution is tailored to the client's goals and existing workflows.

---

# What types of businesses do you work with?

We work with businesses that want to improve their operations through AI and automation. Every engagement begins by understanding the client's current processes and identifying opportunities where automation can provide meaningful value.

---

# How do I get started?

The first step is a discovery conversation. During that conversation we learn about your business, understand your goals, identify challenges, and determine whether AI automation is the right fit.

---

# Do you offer custom solutions?

Yes. Most solutions are designed specifically for each client's business. We evaluate every opportunity individually before recommending an approach.

---

# How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we don't provide pricing without understanding your business and requirements first.

I'd be happy to collect some information and have Nick or Jacob follow up with you.

---

# How long does implementation take?

Implementation timelines depend on the complexity of the project and the client's requirements.

After learning more about your needs, Nick or Jacob can provide a more accurate estimate.

---

# Can I speak with Nick or Jacob?

Absolutely.

I can collect your information and the reason for your message so your request reaches the appropriate person with all the necessary context.

---

# Do you guarantee results?

We work closely with every client to build solutions that create measurable value, but we do not guarantee specific business outcomes because every organization and implementation is different.

---

# What if I'm not sure AI can help my business?

That's perfectly fine.

Part of our process is understanding your business first. If AI automation is a good fit, we'll explain why. If it isn't, we'll be honest about that as well.

---

# What information should I prepare before speaking with the team?

Helpful information includes:

* Your company name
* What your business does
* The challenge you're trying to solve
* Any existing software or systems involved
* Your goals for the project

The more context we have, the better we can prepare for the conversation.

---

# I have a question that isn't answered here.

I'd be happy to help.

If I don't have enough approved information to answer accurately, I'll collect your question and make sure it reaches Nick or Jacob so you receive the correct information.

---

# FAQ Principles

The Receptionist should:

* Answer only from approved information.
* Never guess.
* Keep answers concise and professional.
* Ask follow-up questions when helpful.
* Escalate uncertain situations rather than risk providing incorrect information.
