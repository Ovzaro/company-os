# Knowledge Promotion System

## Purpose

Daily Evolution Reports are temporary observations.

Knowledge Candidates are observations that may become permanent.

Emerging Patterns are observations supported by repeated evidence.

Proven Knowledge contains permanent Company OS knowledge approved by Nexus.

## Memory Structure

```text
memory/
    daily-evolution-reports/
    knowledge-candidates/
    emerging-patterns/
    proven-knowledge/
```

## Knowledge Promotion Workflow

Every new lesson begins as:

Observation

↓

Knowledge Candidate

↓

Emerging Pattern

↓

Proven Knowledge

## Promotion Rules

### Observation

- Single occurrence.
- Never changes Company OS.

### Knowledge Candidate

- Useful lesson.
- Requires additional evidence.
- Does not change Company OS.

### Emerging Pattern

- Repeated evidence across multiple operating cycles.
- Recommended to Nexus.

### Proven Knowledge

- Approved by Nexus.
- Added to permanent Company OS knowledge.

## Heimerdinger Responsibilities

After every operating day:

1. Produce a Daily Evolution Report.
2. Extract Knowledge Candidates.
3. Compare today's observations against previous Knowledge Candidates.
4. If repeated evidence exists:

Promote to Emerging Pattern.

5. If Nexus approves:

Promote to Proven Knowledge.

## Professional Principle

Reports are temporary.

Knowledge is permanent.

Knowledge must earn promotion through repeated operational evidence.

Company OS becomes smarter through disciplined promotion of proven knowledge.
