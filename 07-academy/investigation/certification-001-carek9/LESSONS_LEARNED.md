# Lessons Learned

## What Worked

The investigation followed authority before convenience. Rachel Breuhaus was selected because the company itself identifies her as CEO and Founder, not because her contact path was easy.

The investigation preserved contact hygiene. No guessed email, guessed phone, or false LinkedIn match was presented as verified.

The investigation maintained a queue and recorded exhausted paths, which made the partial status visible instead of hiding it.

## What Did Not Work

LinkedIn discovery failed from available public search results.

RocketReach verification could not be completed because there was no reliable LinkedIn URL and no authenticated RocketReach web app session in this environment.

No secondary named operator or department leader was verified.

## Operational Lesson

For Academy certification, the evaluator should distinguish reasoning quality from completion quality.

This investigation reasoned professionally, but it did not complete the mandatory enrichment workflow. The correct senior review outcome is therefore FAIL, with status partial, not completed.
