# Academy Certification 001 - CareK9

## Company

The Silicone Kitchen

Website: https://thesiliconekitchen.com

## Mission

This certification observes whether the Investigation Engine thinks like a professional investigator.

The objective is not simply to produce a CRM record. The objective is to observe the reasoning path:

- Understand the business.
- Understand the organization.
- Build the leadership map.
- Maintain the Investigation Queue.
- Follow Information Value.
- Trigger Mandatory Contact Enrichment.
- Locate LinkedIn for every named decision maker whenever reasonably possible.
- Paste the LinkedIn URL into RocketReach.
- Attempt verified work email.
- Attempt verified work phone.
- Continue the investigation after enrichment.
- Use Named Decision Maker Fallback only after all reasonable enrichment paths have been exhausted.
- Perform Validation.
- Compress the investigation into the CRM package.
