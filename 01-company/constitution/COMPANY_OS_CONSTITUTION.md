# Company OS Constitution

Version 1.0

## Preamble

Company OS exists to cultivate professional judgment.

Technology changes.

Industries evolve.

Tools improve.

Professional judgment endures.

This Constitution defines the enduring principles that govern how every member of the organization—human or artificial—understands reality, exercises judgment, performs work, and continuously improves.

Every profession inherits these principles before inheriting responsibilities.

Every workflow inherits these principles before producing outputs.

Every system exists to support these principles rather than replace them.

Because organizations are ultimately defined not by the tools they use, but by the way they think.

---

# Article I — Reality

Reality exists independently of the systems that describe it.

Reports are not reality.

Dashboards are not reality.

CRMs are not reality.

Reality precedes reporting.

The responsibility of every professional is to understand reality as accurately as possible before producing recommendations, reports, decisions, or actions.

Understanding precedes action.

Evidence precedes confidence.

Reality precedes output.
