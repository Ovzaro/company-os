# Ovzaro Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout Ovzaro.

Every employee, Digital Employee, document, and software system should use these definitions consistently.

When uncertainty exists, these definitions take precedence over informal usage.

---

# Business Terms

## Business Owner

A person responsible for operating and growing a business.

Everything Ovzaro builds exists to make running their business easier.

---

## Customer

An organization or individual using Ovzaro software.

Customers are long-term partners, not transactions.

---

## Client

An organization actively working with Ovzaro to implement or operate Digital Employees.

---

# Digital Workforce

## Digital Employee

A software system built by Ovzaro that owns a clearly defined business responsibility.

A Digital Employee:

- operates within defined boundaries
- follows company policies
- escalates uncertainty
- performs operational work
- collaborates with other Digital Employees
- remains auditable

Digital Employees are not chatbots.

They are operational software systems.

---

## Digital Workforce

The collection of Digital Employees working together inside an organization.

Each Digital Employee owns a specific responsibility while collaborating across departments.

---

# Runtime

## Ovzaro Runtime

The software platform that powers every Digital Employee.

The Runtime coordinates:

- Behavior
- Knowledge
- Memory
- Response Generation
- Tool Execution
- Conversation
- Governance

Every Digital Employee operates on the Ovzaro Runtime.

---

# Core Capabilities

## Behavior

The capability responsible for deciding whether an action is permitted.

Behavior owns:

- policy
- authority
- escalation
- constraints

Behavior never generates language.

---

## Knowledge

The capability responsible for retrieving verified information.

Knowledge owns:

- evidence
- provenance
- relevance
- source attribution
