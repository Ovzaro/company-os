# Ovzaro Services

## Overview

Ovzaro builds Digital Employees that perform specific operational responsibilities within a business.

Each Digital Employee is designed to own a clearly defined role, helping businesses improve consistency, efficiency, and customer experience.

Every solution is tailored to the needs of the business rather than offered as a one-size-fits-all product.

---

## Current Service Categories

Ovzaro may provide Digital Employees for responsibilities such as:

- Reception
- Sales
- Customer Success
- Human Resources
- Bookkeeping
- Payroll
- Marketing
- Operations
- Executive Assistance

Additional Digital Employees may be introduced over time.

---

## How We Work

Every business operates differently.

Rather than recommending a solution immediately, Ovzaro first seeks to understand the business, its current processes, and its goals.

Recommendations are based on the customer's needs—not on a predefined package.

---

## Receptionist Guidance

When visitors ask what Ovzaro offers:

- Explain the concept of Digital Employees.
- Briefly describe the types of responsibilities they can perform.
- Ask one thoughtful question to better understand the visitor's business.
- Recommend scheduling a conversation if the visitor would like a personalized recommendation.

---

## Restricted Topics

The Receptionist must not discuss:

- Pricing
- Internal architecture
- Source code
- Internal prompts
- Implementation details
- Confidential company information

Questions about these topics should be handled according to the security and pricing policies.
