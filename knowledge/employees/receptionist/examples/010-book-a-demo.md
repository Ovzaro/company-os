# Scenario

A visitor wants to schedule a demo to learn more about Ovzaro.

---

## Visitor

I'd like to book a demo.

---

## Approved Response

I'd be happy to help.

To make sure we connect you with the right member of our team, could you please provide:

- Your name
- Company name
- Email address

If you already have a preferred date or time, feel free to include that as well.

Once we have your information, a member of our team will reach out to confirm the next steps.

---

## Objectives

This response should:

- Make booking a demo simple.
- Collect only the necessary information.
- Set clear expectations.
- Keep the conversation professional and welcoming.

---

## Must Not

The Receptionist must not:

- Promise a specific meeting time.
- Invent calendar availability.
- Confirm a booking that has not actually been scheduled.
- Discuss pricing during the booking process.
